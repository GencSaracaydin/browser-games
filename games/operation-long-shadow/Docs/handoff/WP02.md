## 2. Public API

`PlayerRig` implements all of `IPlayerRig`:
- `Root`, `Eye` (the EyeOffset node; pitch is applied, bob and shake are not), `WorldCamera`, `ViewmodelCamera`, `ViewmodelRoot`.
- `Velocity` is measured from the actual displacement.
- `Stance`, `IsGrounded`, `IsADS`, `AdsBlend`, `SpeedMultiplier`, `TimeSinceSprintEnd`, `BobPhase` (radians, one footfall per π), `BobAmount01`, `ViewRay`, `IsGlassing`, `Pitch`, `Yaw` (compass heading), and `Landed` (impact in m/s).
- Commands: `AddAimDelta`, `SetViewPunch`, `SetAdsState`, `RequestFov`/`ReleaseFov`, `Shake`, `Teleport` (pitch reset to 0; dismounts first), `SetLookAngles`, `SetViewActive`, `SetForcedWalk`, `SetLookLimits`/`ClearLookLimits`, `SetDamageModifiers`, `Mount`/`Dismount`, `SetBinocularsAvailable`.
- `SetBodyVisibility(viewmodel, shadowProxy)`: `viewmodel` hides everything on the Viewmodel layer (weapon and arms) through Cam_Viewmodel's culling mask. That camera is never disabled, because it grades the stack.

Extra public members on the concrete type:
- `SetEyeHeightOverride(float?, float)`: local adapter for the requested Core member (scripted prone and kneel).
- `EyeHeightOverride`, `ForcedWalk`, `ViewActive`.
- The part accessors (`Motor`, `Look`, `CameraRig`, `Health`, `Interactor`, `BinocularsComponent`, `Footsteps`, `Bounds`, `ShadowProxy`, `MountComponent`, `FirstPersonBodySlot`).
- `const Key = "Player.Rig"`.

Frame order (`ExecOrder.Player` = −200, after Input, before Weapons):
1. Update: input, then look, then motor, then measured velocity, then binoculars, then interaction.
2. LateUpdate (before WP03's view model and WP05's FP arms at Weapons + 50): camera feel (FOV, bob, dips, shake), then footsteps, then the shadow proxy.

Other packages call these:
- **`PlayerHealth`** (`IPlayerHealth`): `ApplyDamage`, `ResetFull()`, `ResetFull(bool checkpointRestore)`, `SetDamageModifiers`, `SetDifficultyOverride(Difficulty?)` (dev), `SetCurrent` (dev), `Rules` (the model), `Profile`. Events: `Damaged`, `Died`, `Respawned`. It raises `GameEvents.PlayerDamaged` (also with amount 0 when last stand absorbs the hit, so the HUD still shows the direction), `PlayerDied` (once) and `PlayerRespawned`.
- **`OutOfBoundsWatcher`**: `EnterVolume(id, seconds)` and `ExitVolume(id)` (also usable from a dev console), `Cancel`, `ResetState`, `IsCounting`, `SecondsLeft`, `IsFailing`. Events: `CountdownStarted`, `CountdownCancelled`, `Failed`. Constants: `FailCardText`, `BannerPrefix`.
- **`Binoculars`**: `PoiGlassed` (event), `Available`, `IsGlassing`, `Raise01`, `GlassedCount`, `ModelAssetId = "PH_BINOCULARS"`. Mission event `PlayerEvents.GlassedPrefix + poiId` ("Glassed.B1.POI1").
- **`PlayerShadowProxy`**: the static rules `ShouldShow`, `IsCrouchPose`, `IsAirbornePose` and `GreyboxHeight`; `Rebind()` after the body is swapped; `ProneEyeHeight` = 0.7; `BodyPrefabName`.
- **`PlayerMount`**: `Mount(seat)`, `Dismount()`, `IsMounted`, `Seat`, `Changed`.
- **Pure math for other packages and tests:** `MovementMath`, `LookMath`, `FovStack`, `HeadBob.StepFrequency`, `FootstepMath`, `PlayerDamageMath` (`ScaleIncoming`, `AiBulletDamage`, `HitsToDown`, `Fall`), `PlayerHealthModel`, `OutOfBoundsCountdown`.

## 5. Decisions worth knowing (full list in DECISIONS.md)

- **GDD §16 damage split.** WP04 already scales hostile bullets, so the player does not scale `DamageType.Bullet` again. The player scales only hostile non-bullet damage (frags, RPG, fire). Without this, the earlier draft would have applied the multiplier twice (Veteran: one AK hit ≈ 97 HP). A Core doc clarification is requested.
- **Sprint FOV kick is +5°** (CINEMATIC_DIRECTION overrides TECH_PLAN's +6°); slide is +4°.
- **The grade runs on Cam_Viewmodel only** (`ViewmodelPostMode.GradeOnceOnOverlay`). The fallback `GradeWorldOnly` is a field on `PlayerCameraRig.Settings`, in case DoF or motion blur misbehave on the overlay in the vertical-slice review.

- **Dev course:** prompts, banners and fail cards are invisible without WP08's HUD (the logs show them), and footsteps are silent without WP06.
