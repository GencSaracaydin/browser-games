## 2. Public APIs

Core interfaces implemented (register in `Awake`):
- `ICombatService` → `CombatService` (on `PF_CombatSystems`): `FireAI`, `ThrowGrenadeAI`, `FireRocket` (launch sound, back-blast, ShotFired/Noise), `Explode` (radius ≥ 9 m uses the vehicle fireball), `SpawnSmoke` (SmokeLOS sphere 4.5 m for the duration, default 20 s), `SpawnPickup` (AK/RPK/COMMANDO only; others never drop), `SpawnAmmoCrate`. Extra (same assembly, reach it through Integration): `CombatService.Current`, `ThrowGrenade(pos, vel, fuse, team, instigator)`, `AnyLiveGrenade`, `ClearTransients()`.
- `IWeaponService` → `WeaponInventory` (on the player rig): loadout, `SetHolstered(owner, bool)` (reference-counted owners), `SelectSlot`, `Give` (`Give("M67", n)` adds frags, -1 = up to 4), `Resupply`, `ResetLoadout`, `MountDoorGun(pivot)`, `DismountDoorGun`, `IsDoorGunMounted`. `ISaveable` key `"Weapons"` registers itself with `ICheckpointService` when it appears; restore tops frags up to 2 (GDD §17) and redraws instantly.
- `IWeaponHudState` → `PlayerWeapon`: crosshair spread/visibility (hidden while ADS ≥ 50 % and while sprinting), magazine/size/reserve/frags, fire mode, reloading, `CookTime01`, `Heat01`/`Overheated` (door gun; `MagazineSize` is 0 while mounted).
- `IWeaponGrips` → `ViewmodelController`: `RightGrip` = a proxy on the view model's `Socket_Grip` oriented with the weapon (forward = barrel, up = top, whatever rotation the art's empties carry), `LeftGrip` = a proxy that sits on the handguard and follows the magazine during reloads, the charging handle/bolt/slide during the charge, and the frag or knife during hand actions; `SupportHint` = left elbow hint; `LeftHandWeight` (pistol sprints one-handed). All null/0 while the weapon is holstered (FPArmsIK relaxes the hands).
- `IDestructible` → `Destructible` (registered in `Destructibles` by id and with the checkpoint service).
- `IInteractable` → `WeaponPickup` (`HOLD [E] Take AK`, 0.4 s), `AmmoCrate` (`HOLD [E] Resupply`, 1.0 s).

Events raised: `ShotFired`, `Noise`, `HitConfirmed` (enemy actors and destructibles), `TracerPath`, `NearMiss`, `GrenadeThrown/Landed/Exploded`, `Explosion`, `WeaponChanged`, `AmmoChanged` (only on change), `DestructibleDamaged/Destroyed` (not re-raised on checkpoint restore). `ActorDamaged/ActorKilled` come from Core `Health`.

Player feel, per shot: hitscan from `IPlayerRig.ViewRay` through the spread cone (500 m, `LayerIds.PlayerShot`, nearest first, cloth and glass penetrated up to twice at ×0.85, bodies and walls stop it) → damage → `AddAimDelta(kick)` + `SetViewPunch(30 % of the kick)` → view-model kick (2.2 cm back, spring) → muzzle flash on the view-model muzzle + 50 ms 4 m light → physical casing → tracer every Nth round from the projected muzzle → `PlayWeaponShot` → events. Recovery returns the aim at the GDD rate after the GDD delay and is paid down when the player pulls against the recoil.

Useful statics: `WeaponTable.Get/GetEnemy/DisplayName/ArtName`, `PlayerWeapon.AdsVerticalFov(spec, hipVFov)`, `WeaponPickups.Spawn/Clear`, `AmmoCrate.Spawn`, `CombatFx.Instance`, `HitscanShooter.Fire`, `CombatResolver.Explode/FireAI/IsCovered`.

## 5. Decisions (also in Docs/DECISIONS.md)

Recoil = aim kick (moves the aim) + an extra 30 % visual camera punch + a 2.2 cm model kick; ADS FOV keeps the GDD magnification at any FOV setting (tangent ratio, exact at the default); no wall penetration (GDD is silent), cloth/glass pass; knife and frag use the left hand (Black Ops), the knife lunge extends reach to 2.6 m without moving the player; enemy tracers 1 in 3 (CINEMATIC_DIRECTION overrides GDD §13.4), squad 1 in 5, door gun every 3rd; reload ammo commits at the magazine seat; sprint, switch, frag and knife cancel a reload; an empty magazine reloads itself on the next trigger press or 0.15 s after the last round; a fresh trigger pull fires at once, long frames release at most 3 rounds; dropped weapons are physical and move into the scene of the ground they lie on (so drops in an unloaded section vanish); world models are named `_W` by the art pipeline (`_World` accepted).

## 6. Known gaps

- Tests and builders have not run in the editor yet (no editor in this phase). Most likely to need a touch: decal shader property names on the WP10 decal material, particle blend setup if URP changes defaults, and the per-gun hip poses once real models are seen.
- No first-person arm animation clips: the reload, frag and knife are procedural (keyed transforms + IK grips), by design (GDD §13.1).
- No separate bolt/charging-handle mesh: the charge is shown by the left hand pulling the handle point and a weapon jolt. A movable part can be added later as a `Bolt` child under the model (not wired yet).
- The door gun's spade-grip hand pose and the frag/knife hand pose belong to WP05.
- The AI never "sees" player bullets passing (no suppression of enemies by the player); not specified by the GDD.
- `IWeaponHudState` has no "low ammo" flag; the HUD derives it from Magazine/MagazineSize (GDD §11 flash below 25 %); Combat plays `SFX_UI_LOW_AMMO` once per crossing.
- Smoke grenades thrown by the squad are a WP04 animation plus `SpawnSmoke`; there is no thrown smoke projectile.
