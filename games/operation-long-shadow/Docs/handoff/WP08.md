### Look (1984 aesthetic)
- HUD: warm off-white ink with a soft dark underlay over the sunlit desert; markers amber #E3A33B → pale green #A6E3B4 from LightStage `s` 2.0, red #E04B3B high priority; condensed numerals (Barlow Condensed), typewriter objectives (Courier Prime) with a dimmed `OBJ 4/11 — 75810 —` prefix and teletype reveal.
- Pause, options, select beat, click gate and debrief: green P1 phosphor on CRT glass, inverse-video selection with a `>` caret, scanlines, vignette, a slow rolling refresh band and flicker (`CrtScreen`). Options sliders are 20-cell phosphor bars; choices are `< VALUE >`.
- Title card: amber phosphor type in Special Elite with a glow and a faint warm bloom, 2.39:1 bars, number groups that resolve into text, the 18:40 → 17:58 flash.
- Debrief: CRT dossier in Courier Prime (columns line up), live counting stats, an animated strike through "HOSTILE", "UNDER REVIEW" typed after, inverse-video redaction blocks wiping across the names, then the stamp.

## 2. Public APIs

- **`HudRoot : MonoBehaviour, IHud`** (PF_HUD; registers `IHud` in Awake, execution order `ExecOrder.UI`). Every IHud member is implemented: `ShowPrompt/HidePrompt` (several prompts by id; the newest shows; `[KEY]` drawn in amber; hold radial from `Progress01`; calling it every frame with the same text is free), `Tutorial(key, text)` (once ever per key: `ls.tut.<key>` through `ISettingsService.SetValue`), `IntelCard(title, photo, counter)` (polaroid, 2.5 s, queued), `FailCard(text)` (holds until `CheckpointLoaded` or 6 s), `Banner(text, seconds)` (`""`/0 clears), `SetOverlay(HudOverlay)`, `SetRing/ClearRing` (up to 4), `Letterbox(on, seconds)`, `SetVisible(bool)`, `TitleCard(seconds)`, `ShowDebrief(stats)` (null = `IMissionDirector.Stats`). Extra: `Initialize(UITheme)` and `TickAll(dt)` (edit-mode tests/tools), `SetEventsEnabled(bool)`, `CollectLayout`, `ForceLayout`, `Views`, `Context`.
- The HUD hides the gameplay layer on `CinematicStarted…Ended`, `InsertStarted…Ended`, while the debrief is open and on `SetVisible(false)`; subtitles, letterbox, title card, banner and fail card stay up.
- **`ClickGateView : MonoBehaviour, IClickGate`** (PF_ClickGate in S_Boot; registers in Awake at `ExecOrder.Platform − 50` so WP01's Bootstrap finds it): `SetProgress` fills 10 five-digit groups, `SetReady(true)` types `SESSION 12 // SUBJECT S-3 // CLICK TO BEGIN` with the blinking block cursor, `Clicked` fires once per showing on the pointer-down (or Enter/Space) only when ready, `SetVisible`. Never requests pointer lock or fullscreen itself. Raises the mission event `UI.Gate.Ready` when it becomes ready while shown.
- **Events the HUD consumes:** `SubtitleShow/Hide`, `HitConfirmed` (the HUD plays `SFX_UI_HITMARKER`, `SFX_UI_HEADSHOT`, `SFX_UI_KILL`), `PlayerDamaged`, `GrenadeLanded/Exploded`, `ObjectivesChanged`, `ObjectiveCompleted`, `MissionEvent` (`Glassed.<POI>` ticks the binocular counter), `PauseChanged`, `SettingsChanged`, `LightStageChanged`, `CheckpointLoaded`, `CinematicStarted/Ended`, `InsertStarted/Ended`.
- **Polled services:** `IPlayerRig` (camera, eye, ADS, stance, glassing), `IWeaponHudState`, `IObjectiveService`, `ILightStage.HudColor`/`StoryClockMinutes`, `IPlayerHealth`, `ISquadService.Member`, `IEncounterService.Actor`, `IInputReader.ObjectivesHeld` (Tab), `ISettingsService`, `IPauseService`, `IMissionDirector`, `ICheckpointService`, `IClickGate`, `IMarkerRegistry` (GlassingPOI count). All optional.
- **Mission events the UI raises** (`UiEvents`; Core copies requested): `UI.TitleCardFinished`, `UI.Gate.Ready`, `UI.Debrief.Ready`, `UI.Debrief.Replay`, `UI.Debrief.End`, `UI.Debrief.Select.<CP>`, `UI.Pause.Quit`, `UI.Pause.Restart`.
- **`UIAdapters`** (static delegates for pending Core members; WP11 assigns them): `RelockCooldown` (Func<float>) and `Awareness01` (Func<string,float>).
- **`UITheme`**: fonts per `FontRole` (+ saved shadow/glow materials), sprite overrides per `UISpriteId`, texture overrides, look switches (glow, scanline strength, flicker, HUD opacity). `UISprites.Get(id, theme)` returns the art or the procedural stand-in.

- **Core requests pending:** `SequenceIds` copies of the UI event ids; `IPauseService.RelockCooldown` and `IEncounterService.Awareness01` (requested by WP01/WP04) are read through `UIAdapters` until they land.
- **Gesture re-arm after QUIT/END/REPLAY** depends on WP01 (request filed). Without it, the second gate click starts the mission without pointer lock and the first lock loss shows the pause menu's click-to-resume. SELECT BEAT deliberately resumes behind the pause menu for the same reason.
- The debrief stamp uses `T_StampAssetsActive_UI_BC` only when the theme finds it; the redaction-bar and dossier-paper art are not used on the CRT (decision logged). `T_UI_Scanlines` is not used (clamped by the import rules; identical procedural tile).
- Font atlases are static and prefilled with `UIFonts.RequiredCharacters` (ASCII + typographic marks used by the dialogue, objectives, title card, debrief and menus; DIALOGUE.json is pure ASCII); anything else falls back to LiberationSans SDF.
- Subtitles longer than two lines at the smallest step are ellipsised (GDD §11 "2 lines"); VO may want to split very long lines.
- The binocular `OBSERVED n/3` counter counts distinct `Glassed.*` events since the last checkpoint load; the total is the number of `GlassingPOI` markers registered (3 when none are).
- Sardani: v2 shipped after rejecting v1 (Arabic-like detached dots); O3 review result is in Docs/DECISIONS.md. Glyphs step above the cap line and below the baseline: give signs about 25 % extra line spacing.
