## 2. Public APIs

**Services (Core).**
- `EncounterSpawner` implements `IEncounterService`: `Activate`, `TriggerWave`, `Deactivate`, `Alive`, `Killed`, `Cleared`, `Actor`, `AliveTotal`. It also exposes `DespawnEnemy(id)`, `Awareness01(id)` (Core request filed), `Runtime(id)`, `RegisterEncounter(def)` and `DespawnAll()`. Its save key is "Encounters" (killed ids).
- `SquadService` implements `ISquadService`, with save key "Squad".
- `TargetRegistry` implements `ITargetRegistry`.

**Events raised.**
- `EncounterActivated`, `WaveSpawned` (when the wave's first member spawns), `EncounterCleared` and `CombatStateChanged`.
- Mission events: `E5.FirstGlint`, `E5.FirstShot`, `B4.TechnicalIgnited`, `B10.RPGAtMagpie`, `B11.RPGLaunch` and `AI.Morale.<enc>.<wave>`.

**Mission events listened to.**
- `AI.Alert.<enc>[.<wave>]`, `AI.Run.<enemyId>`, `B4.TechnicalOpen`, `B6.Blast` and `MIDA.Start`.
- `TriggerEntered`, `Interacted` and `DestructibleDestroyed` ids, plus `VOLineStarted` as `VO:<lineId>`. These feed the wave conditions.

All AI event ids live in `AIEvents` and are requested for `SequenceIds`.

**Rules as pure functions** (tested): `HitChance.Compute`, `KillShare.IsLethal`, `CalloutDirection.Classify`, `CalloutIds.Spot`, `SquadCallouts.LineFor`, `MoraleRule.ShouldBreak`, `SpawnRules.ClosetAllowed/UnderCaps`, `PerceptionMath.*`, `SentryAttention.Scale`, `DoorGunArc.Contains`, `ExfilFlightPath.Sample*`, `SquadFollowPlanner.*`, `SquadLookAt.Choose` and `LinkTraversal.ActionFor`.

**Bodies.**
- Every brain drives an `ICharacterBody`. It uses Core `ActionIds` (Reload, Throw, RpgRaise, Vault/Climb/Drop/LadderClimb, Photograph, BoltCut and the rest) plus `AIActionIds.SmokeIdle`/`Stop` (Core request filed).
- It sends `SendMessage("SetPerch", bool)` (a WP05 hook).
- It reads `Muzzle`, `RightHand` and `Head` for shots, throws, drops and eye position.
- `DebugCapsuleBody.Build(go, team, materialFn, squad)` makes a greybox soldier: a 1.8 m capsule, head/torso/leg hitboxes, a muzzle and an agent. It moves in straight lines when there is no NavMesh. Loop actions complete at once; unknown actions log once and complete.

## 3. Editor steps for the integrator (editor holder)

1. Start of slot: run `land.py --status`, refresh, then check that the console shows 0 errors.
2. After WP05's **LongShadow/Characters/Build All** (bodies `PFB_LegionA|B|C`, `PFB_GreycoatA|B|B_NVG`, `PFB_Hollis|Sparrow|Tinker`), run **LongShadow/AI/Build All AI Prefabs**. This runs Build Enemy Prefabs, Build AI Services Prefab and Build Squad Prefabs.
   - The log line `EnemyPrefabBuilder: N enemy prefabs (x on WP05 bodies, y on DebugCapsuleBody)` says how many bodies were found.
   - Re-run it whenever WP05 rebuilds bodies: rebuilt bodies get new internal ids.
   - It also works before WP05 lands, on capsule soldiers.
3. WP11 places `Prefabs/Enemies/PF_AIServices.prefab` and `Prefabs/Squad/PF_Squad.prefab` in `S_Persistent`. Keep `LongShadow.AI.Enemy.EnemyLibrary` and `EnemyPrefabEntry` in `link.xml`.
4. Run **LongShadow/AI/Validate Encounter Ids**. It checks every id the AI uses against `markup_ids.csv` and counts the ones present in the open scenes. It should report 0 missing from the CSV.
5. Run **LongShadow/AI/Build AI Dev Arena**, then press Play in `Scenes/Dev/AIDevArena.unity`. **LongShadow/AI/Open AI Dev Arena** reopens it later.
   - The overlay (top left) shows AI ms avg/peak against the 3 ms budget, the ray peak against 24, tokens held against capacity, alive counts and every enemy's state, mode and token.
   - The buttons drive the squad (node set A/B, follow, chemlights) and the waves (Alert, Wave B, Wave G, Restart).
   - The dummy player patrols, crouches, makes footstep noise and fires back.
   - For the DoD profiler check, open the Unity Profiler (markers `LS.AI.Tick` and `LS.AI.Perception`) with 7 enemies and 3 squad alive.
6. EditMode tests: `TestRunnerBridge.RunEditMode("LongShadow.Tests.EditMode.AI")`. Expected: all green, including the 15 tests that need the editor. Clear the console afterwards.

## 5. Known gaps and notes

- **Not verified in the editor yet:** nothing has been landed or run in Unity, since that is the LAND agent's and editor holder's slot. The builders, the arena scene, NavMesh baking and prefab-instance unpacking are untested in a live editor. They use standard APIs, but give them one careful look on the first run.
- **AI cost ≤ 3 ms:** measure it in the arena (see §3 step 5). Perception runs at 10 Hz under the 24-ray budget, decisions at 5 Hz, and the per-frame brain work is allocation-free after warm-up apart from rare barks and events.
- **Deviation:** E2 spawns all six placed enemies at load (2 sentries plus a gate team of 4), exceeding its max alive of 4, because GDD §7 says placed enemies exist when the beat loads. Max alive governs every reinforcement.
- **Closet patience:** after 20 s blocked, a closet spawn relaxes the 15 m rule to 10 m, never the sight rule. This exists to prevent soft-locks.
- **Scripted pieces left to others:**
  - BK_GRY_09 at FL1, BK_LEG_08 at the floods, D-line cues and the R3-nest squad kill are WP07's.
  - The E11 trucks (20 hits, fireball) are WP03 destructibles on WP10 splines. `ExfilSchedule` has only their path, for the arc test.
- **Not built:** PlayMode tests (`Staging/Tests/PlayMode/AI` is empty). The dev arena is the play-mode check.
- **Placeholder visuals:** the scope glint, NVG lens and chemlight are simple HDR quads/spheres with point lights. They are good enough to read at distance, but they are not authored VFX. WP10 could replace them with flare sprites.
- **Squad weapons:** every member carries the Commando, because the GDD does not specify their guns.
- **Animation-dependent behaviour:** the sentries smoke only once WP05 maps `SmokeIdle`; until then they stand. Cover poses (`AnimatorParams.Cover` 0–4) and perch idles depend on AC_Soldier.
