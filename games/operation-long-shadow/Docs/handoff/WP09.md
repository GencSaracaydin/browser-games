## 5. Known gaps and risks
- **Not executed in the editor.** Shaders (no offline compiler), builders (scene/prefab/timeline/profile asset APIs) and tests have only been compile-checked. Expect a first-run fix-up slot. Riskiest: the CRT forward pass (Forward+ reflection-probe keywords), the UI shaders on the overlay canvas, removing LODGroups/adding children on FBX prefab instances in the booth builder, `Lightmapping.SetLightingSettingsForScene` on an additively opened scene.
- **Glitch pass on camera stacks**: the renderer feature also runs on the view-model overlay camera, so the world gets the RGB split twice during gameplay inserts (subtle).
- **MID-B shot 1** frames the projector table instead of FP hands threading the film (no IFirstPersonBody action; requested). The gloved hand of intro shot 7 is sold by sound, the forced head turn and an impulse (no "reach to head" action); the interrogator walks behind the chair but is off-frame.
- **Insertion**: no cabin interior inside the Tripo exterior (WP10_FAL known issue) — I-3/I-4 rely on 35/85 mm shallow DoF; WP10's `VEH_HELI_CABIN` prefab is looked up (`LIB_Cinematics` id VEH_HELI_CABIN) but not attached yet. Cast bodies are moved on rails (TransformPath) with looping actions, so feet can slide on steep ground; the knot heights of INS.Heli.Approach are partly below terrain and get lifted to 7 m clearance (verify the approach reads "low over the dunes").
- **Booth lighting** relies on realtime lights (key + fill + 6 CRT glows + lighter); 9 small lights in a booth-only view are within budget. Unbaked booths have no probe ambient on the bodies (dark but correct); bake per §3 step 4.
- **Subtitle-only fallback** durations come from the envelopes or a measured table; if VO is retaken, re-run Build Timelines.
- **SFX_PEN_TAP_1** is requested from WP06; until then the per-digit taps in the outro are silent.
- The death insert's own "SESSION 12 — RESUMING" only shows without a HUD; with WP07+WP08 the banner is theirs.
- `IHud.TitleCard` is assumed to draw its own bars and teletype with the HUD visible (REQUESTS.md asks WP08 to hide gameplay widgets meanwhile).
- Core requests (CORE_CHANGES.md): `StorageKeys.CinematicSeenPrefix`, optional `IScreenFX.FadeToWhite`; local adapters in place.
